Summary

This gene exhibits pronounced tissue-specific expression patterns in Anopheles gambiae, with highest abundance in posterior midgut, Malpighian tubules, and whole body tissues.
  • Expression shows dramatic body versus head enrichment (>6 log2-fold difference) with robust circadian oscillation in bodies peaking at ZT16-20
  • Strong female-biased expression pattern observed across multiple experiments
  • Blood feeding triggers rapid downregulation (3-72 hours post-feeding) followed by recovery to baseline by 15 days, suggesting negative regulation during active digestion
  • Expression is markedly suppressed in chemosensory tissues (antennae, maxillary palps) and salivary glands
  • Shows developmental regulation with peak expression during early embryogenesis (8 hours post-fertilization) and late larval stages
The tissue distribution and circadian rhythmicity suggest roles in metabolic processes, osmoregulation, or detoxification that cycle with daily physiological rhythms.
