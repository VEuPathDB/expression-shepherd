Summary

This gene exhibits extreme tissue-specific expression patterns across multiple Anopheles gambiae organs and developmental stages.
  • Expression is dramatically elevated in salivary glands (up to 98,367 TPM, 100th percentile), male testes (12,591-12,633 TPM, 99.99th percentile), and adult carcasses (4,815-6,229 TPM, >99th percentile) compared to other tissues.
  • The gene shows strong enrichment in anterior digestive tract regions (proventriculus/anterior midgut, >85,000 TPM) versus posterior midgut (~400 TPM).
  • Expression is female-biased in adults (~250-fold increase from larvae to females) but male-biased in whole body samples.
  • The gene displays transient suppression at 3 hours post-blood meal with rapid recovery by 24 hours.
The extreme salivary gland enrichment and tissue-specific patterns suggest critical roles in secretory functions, potentially related to blood-feeding, digestion, and pathogen transmission.
