Summary

This gene exhibits dramatic temporal regulation during early embryonic development, peaking at 4-5 hours post-egg laying with >100-fold upregulation compared to adult levels, before declining sharply.
  • Expression is highly enriched in midgut tissue across multiple life stages, showing >350-fold higher levels than salivary glands and strong posterior-to-anterior expression gradients along the alimentary canal.
  • The gene shows modest upregulation following mating in female midguts and strong downregulation after blood feeding.
  • Testis-specific expression is observed in males, with 9-fold higher levels than accessory glands.
The embryonic timing and midgut enrichment suggest potential roles in early developmental patterning and digestive physiology, possibly related to nutrient processing or epithelial function.
