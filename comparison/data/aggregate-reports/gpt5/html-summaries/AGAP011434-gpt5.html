Summary

Overview
  • Expression is strongly enriched in larvae of Anopheles gambiae, while adults (carcass and reproductive tissues) are near baseline.
  • Larval tissues: highest in posterior midgut and hindgut; lower in gastric caeca, anterior midgut, and salivary glands; whole larvae are intermediate.
  • Adults show a sharp, transient induction ~3 h after a blood meal, then a return to baseline.
  • Otherwise, adult tissues are low; midgut exceeds salivary gland but absolute levels are modest.
  • These patterns suggest a larval gut-associated role in digestion or nutrient handling, with a brief role early after blood feeding in adults.
