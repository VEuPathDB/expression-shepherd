Summary

Overview
  • Adult tissues: extremely high in midgut (peak in anterior; midgut > salivary gland) and high in bodies/carcass; low in heads and chemosensory appendages.
  • Life stage: strongly adult‑biased, with adults—especially female carcass—far exceeding larvae and early post‑oviposition.
  • Dynamics: transient suppression 3 h after a blood meal with later rebound; ivermectin elevates relative to blood‑fed; circadian oscillations are modest; levels rise by day 20.
  • Reproductive/immune: testes >> male accessory glands; 20‑hydroxyecdysone strongly induces spermatheca; hemocytes upregulate with infectious parasites.
  • Functional hint: pattern suggests a highly abundant midgut digestive or barrier protein modulated by feeding and hormonal cues.
