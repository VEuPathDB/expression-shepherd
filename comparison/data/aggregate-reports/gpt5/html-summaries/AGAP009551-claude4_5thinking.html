Summary

This gene exhibits dramatic tissue-specific expression, reaching the 99th percentile in male Malpighian tubules and showing strong enrichment in midgut tissues of Anopheles gambiae, suggesting critical roles in excretion and digestion. Expression is strongly male-biased in multiple tissues and displays robust circadian rhythmicity in mosquito bodies. The gene is markedly downregulated following blood feeding (~5-6 fold reduction within 3 hours) and shows enhanced translational activation during Plasmodium falciparum infection. Additional notable patterns include early embryonic expression peaks (6-8 hours post-fertilization), upregulation during desiccation stress, and differential expression between incipient A. gambiae species (S form vs M form). The expression profile suggests involvement in osmoregulation, metabolic processes, and physiological responses to environmental and reproductive challenges.
