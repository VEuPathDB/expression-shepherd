Summary

This gene exhibits dramatic tissue-specific enrichment in Anopheles gambiae salivary glands (98-99th percentile, >100-fold higher than midgut) and shows complex developmental regulation.
  • Expression is minimal in early embryos (2-7h) but undergoes sharp upregulation at 8-10h post-fertilization, maintaining high levels through late embryogenesis and larval stages
  • Expression drops markedly during pupation but rebounds in adults
  • The gene responds strongly to blood feeding (5-7 fold upregulation) and increases substantially with adult age (8-fold from day 0 to day 10)
  • Additional enrichment occurs in fat body, carcass tissues, and male chemosensory appendages (maxillary palps)
  • Expression is associated with bendiocarb insecticide resistance
The salivary gland specialization, metabolic tissue enrichment, and blood feeding responsiveness suggest potential roles in vector competence, nutrient metabolism, or host-vector interactions.
