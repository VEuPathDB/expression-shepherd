Summary

This gene exhibits dramatic tissue-specific expression patterns in Anopheles gambiae.
  • Shows extreme enrichment in salivary glands (up to 87,000 TPM, 99.9th percentile) and anterior digestive regions including proventriculus and anterior midgut
  • Displays strong male-biased expression in whole body samples and midgut tissue
  • Exhibits adult-specific upregulation with minimal expression in embryos and larvae
  • Demonstrates substantial upregulation following mating in both male testes (10-fold increase) and female reproductive tissues
  • Shows circadian oscillation in head tissue with peak expression at subjective dusk
The exceptionally high expression in salivary glands and sugar-processing digestive regions, combined with blood-feeding responsiveness, suggests roles in digestion, saliva production, or potentially pathogen transmission biology.
