Dramatic blood meal-induced expression in female midgut suggests critical role in blood digestion

Summary

This gene exhibits highly specific and dramatic upregulation in the midgut of female Anopheles gambiae mosquitoes following blood feeding.
  • Expression increases approximately 100-fold within 24 hours post-blood meal, reaching peak levels (up to 15,000 TPM) before returning to baseline
  • Shows strong midgut-specific expression (96th-99th percentile) in tissue atlases, with minimal expression in other organs
  • Displays circadian rhythmic expression in body tissues under both light-dark and constant dark conditions
  • Associated with insecticide resistance in multiple field populations
  • Exhibits male-biased expression in non-blood-feeding contexts and increases with male aging
The consistent blood meal responsiveness and extreme midgut specificity strongly suggest this gene plays an essential role in blood digestion processes.
