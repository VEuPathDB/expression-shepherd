Summary

This gene exhibits pronounced tissue-specific expression in the Anopheles gambiae midgut and other digestive tissues, ranking consistently above the 97th percentile. Expression is dramatically elevated in adult females compared to larvae (approximately 32-fold) and shows strong upregulation in insecticide-resistant populations and following ivermectin treatment.
  • Blood feeding triggers transient downregulation followed by recovery
  • Expression displays circadian rhythmicity in body tissues
  • The gene shows moderate enrichment in male testes and responds to mating in female reproductive tissues
The convergence of midgut specificity, xenobiotic responsiveness, and insecticide resistance association suggests this gene likely encodes a detoxification enzyme involved in digestion and metabolic defense against chemical insults.
