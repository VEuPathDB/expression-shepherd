Summary

This gene displays strong tissue-specific expression patterns with highest levels in chemosensory organs, particularly maxillary palps and salivary glands. Expression is consistently downregulated following blood feeding (~25% reduction sustained through 15 days) and shows age-related decline in both sexes over 30 days post-eclosion.
  • Developmental regulation occurs during embryogenesis with 2-fold upregulation from early to late stages
  • Male expression generally exceeds female levels across tissues
  • Reproductive organs show notably low expression compared to somatic tissues
The strong chemosensory organ expression combined with blood meal responsiveness suggests potential involvement in host-seeking behavior or feeding regulation, possibly functioning as a metabolic sensor that modulates mosquito behavioral states.
