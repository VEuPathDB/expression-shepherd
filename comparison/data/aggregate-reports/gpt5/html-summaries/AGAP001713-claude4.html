Summary

This gene exhibits pronounced tissue-specific expression patterns across Anopheles gambiae development and anatomy.
  • Dramatically upregulated in salivary glands versus midguts (7-15 fold higher)
  • Shows strong developmental stage-specific expression, with highest levels in adult females compared to larvae
  • Displays circadian rhythmicity with body tissues expressing higher levels than head tissues
  • Exhibits sex-specific differences, particularly elevated in male maxillary palps
  • Responds to blood feeding with upregulation and shows modulation by various treatments including insecticide resistance
The consistent salivary gland enrichment and developmental regulation suggest this gene may encode a salivary protein involved in adult feeding behavior, potentially related to blood meal processing or host interaction mechanisms.
