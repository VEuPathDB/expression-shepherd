Summary

This gene exhibits exceptional female reproductive tissue specificity in Anopheles gambiae.
  • Expression is dramatically enriched in ovaries (70th-89th percentile) and female whole body compared to male tissues (>295-fold difference)
  • Functions as a maternal transcript, with peak expression immediately after egg laying (~50 TPM at 0hr) followed by rapid degradation within 6-8 hours
  • Shows strong upregulation following blood feeding, peaking at 48-72 hours post-bloodmeal (~3-fold increase), coinciding with vitellogenesis and the gonotrophic cycle
  • Expression is enhanced in polysome fractions during Plasmodium falciparum infection, suggesting translational activation during parasite invasion
The temporal and spatial expression patterns strongly suggest this gene plays a critical role in oogenesis, egg development, and maternal provisioning of early embryos.
