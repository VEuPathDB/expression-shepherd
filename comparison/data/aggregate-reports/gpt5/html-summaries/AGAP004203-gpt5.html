AGAP004203 (vitellogenin)

Female‑biased somatic transcript with dramatic post‑blood‑meal surges in Anopheles gambiae

Model: GPT-5

Summary

Summary:
  • Strong female-biased expression in Anopheles gambiae somatic tissues: high in female carcass, fat body, head and salivary glands; moderate in ovaries; very low in males and larvae.
  • Blood-meal responsive: minimal at baseline but explosively and transiently induced 3–24 h post-feeding across whole body, fat body and antenna, returning toward baseline by 48–96 h.
  • Mating/hormone regulation: increased in lower reproductive tract after mating (6–12 h peak) yet strongly repressed in spermatheca and after 20E injection.
  • Parasite interaction: transient midgut induction during invasion; with P. falciparum, total mRNA falls while polysome association rises, implying selective translation.
  • Together, patterns suggest a post‑prandial, female‑focused role, potentially in metabolic or secreted processes supporting feeding and reproduction.
