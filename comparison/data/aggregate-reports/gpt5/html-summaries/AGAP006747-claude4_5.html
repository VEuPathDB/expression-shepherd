Chemosensory and male reproductive tissue enrichment with upregulation in insecticide resistance and thermal stress response

Summary

This gene exhibits pronounced tissue-specific expression in Anopheles gambiae with notable enrichment in chemosensory organs and male reproductive tissues.
  • Expression is highest in male maxillary palps (117 TPM) and antennae (97 TPM), suggesting roles in male chemosensation and host-seeking behavior
  • Testes show 3-fold higher expression than male accessory glands, indicating involvement in spermatogenesis
  • The gene is significantly upregulated in multi-insecticide resistant populations (~1.8-fold, 99th percentile) and shows genotype-specific upregulation during heat shock in larvae with the 2La-standard chromosomal arrangement
  • Downregulation occurs in deltamethrin-resistant mosquitoes (~2.4-fold) and following blood feeding, while Wolbachia wMel infection induces strong upregulation
The combined chemosensory, reproductive, and stress-response patterns suggest this gene may function in sensory-mediated behaviors and adaptive stress responses.
