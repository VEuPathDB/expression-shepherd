Summary

This gene exhibits striking larval-stage specificity with pronounced spatial compartmentalization in the alimentary canal.
  • Expression is dramatically upregulated in Anopheles gambiae larvae (37-92 TPM in L1-L3 instars) compared to negligible levels in adults and embryos (<2 TPM)
  • Within larvae, expression shows >2-fold enrichment in posterior midgut (98th percentile) and hindgut (97th percentile) relative to anterior gut regions
  • A transient peak occurs at 3 hours post-blood meal in adult females (93rd percentile), rapidly returning to baseline, suggesting involvement in immediate digestive responses
  • Late embryonic upregulation occurs at 43-46 hours before hatching, potentially indicating roles in larval transition
The combined larval specificity and posterior gut localization suggest specialized functions in nutrient absorption, waste processing, or metamorphosis-related physiological remodeling in the digestive tract.
