AGAP003751 (cuticular protein gene)

Salivary/proventriculus enrichment with strong post‑blood‑meal repression and stage‑dependent dynamics in Anopheles gambiae

Summary

Overview
  • In Anopheles gambiae, expression is highest in carcass and secretory/epithelial tissues, notably salivary glands and the proventriculus, and minimal in midgut regions and female reproductive tissues.
  • Feeding status strongly modulates abundance: it is high in non‑blood‑fed adults but is robustly and persistently repressed after a blood meal; parasite exposure elevates midgut levels.
  • Developmentally, transcripts rise across embryogenesis, are higher in larvae and virgin females than in gravid females, and decline with adult age.
  • Mating triggers clear upregulation in the spermatheca.
Functional note
  • This pattern suggests roles in secretory/barrier epithelia and pre‑prandial physiology, potentially linked to salivary/proventricular function, with regulation by feeding and reproductive status.
