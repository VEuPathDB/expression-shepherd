Highly expressed digestive tract protein with mating-dependent regulation

Summary

This gene exhibits extremely high tissue-specific expression in digestive organs, particularly the proventriculus (35,270 TPM) and midgut regions of Anopheles gambiae. Expression patterns reveal dramatic mating-dependent regulation, with 28-fold upregulation in male testes post-mating and substantial increases in female reproductive tissues following mating. The gene shows strong developmental restriction to adults and demonstrates sexual dimorphism with female-biased expression in whole body samples. Notably, Wolbachia infection dramatically suppresses expression (35-55 fold reduction), while the gene maintains high expression in salivary glands and hemocytes. The digestive tract localization combined with mating-responsive regulation suggests this protein may function in nutrient processing or metabolic transitions associated with reproduction.
