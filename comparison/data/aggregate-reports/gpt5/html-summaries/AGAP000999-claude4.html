Summary

This gene exhibits highly tissue-specific expression with strongest activity in reproductive tissues, particularly ovaries and salivary glands. Expression patterns reveal circadian rhythms and dramatic responses to blood feeding, with sustained upregulation throughout the gonotrophic cycle. The gene shows female-biased expression across developmental stages and peaks during early embryogenesis and egg laying. Notable expression occurs in chemosensory organs with sex-specific differences, and the gene responds to various environmental stresses including Plasmodium infection and insecticide exposure. The expression profile suggests potential roles in
  • reproductive physiology and oogenesis
  • blood meal processing and metabolism
  • circadian-regulated processes
  • chemosensory functions
