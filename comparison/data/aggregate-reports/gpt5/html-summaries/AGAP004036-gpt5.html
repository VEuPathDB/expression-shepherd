Ovary-enriched, maternally loaded transcript with strong post–blood meal induction in Anopheles gambiae

Summary

Summary
  • Ovary-enriched and female-biased in Anopheles gambiae: high in ovaries and female reproductive tissues; very low in males, larvae, and most somatic tissues.
  • Maternal dynamics: a strong surge immediately after oviposition (0–4 h) followed by rapid decline by ~6–10 h, consistent with maternal mRNA loading and clearance during early embryogenesis.
  • Blood feeding: robust induction after a blood meal, peaking at 24–72 h and remaining elevated; ovaries show the highest levels.
  • Pathogen/other signals: Plasmodium falciparum infection increases polysomal association (suggesting translational upregulation); a low-level, fat body–restricted small RNA is detected from this locus.
  • Together, these patterns suggest a role in oogenesis and early embryo development, aligned with reproductive physiology.
