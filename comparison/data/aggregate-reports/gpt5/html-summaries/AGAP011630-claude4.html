Summary

This gene exhibits dramatically tissue-specific expression with highest levels in male reproductive organs, particularly testes (~2,264 TPM vs 16.4 TPM in accessory glands).
  • Shows strong upregulation in response to blood feeding and malaria parasite infection, with infected mosquitoes reaching >6,000 TPM compared to <100 TPM in controls
  • Maintains high expression in digestive tissues including salivary glands, proventriculus, and midgut compartments
  • Exhibits developmental regulation with peak expression in adults and virtual absence in early embryos
The extreme tissue specificity combined with dramatic infection responses suggests this gene likely encodes a reproductive or digestive enzyme that becomes highly upregulated during blood meal processing and parasite development, possibly functioning in sperm production, digestion, or host-parasite interactions.
