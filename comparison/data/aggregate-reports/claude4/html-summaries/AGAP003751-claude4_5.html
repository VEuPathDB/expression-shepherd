Tissue-Specific Expression with Salivary Gland Enrichment and Reproductive Cycle Downregulation

Summary

This gene exhibits pronounced tissue-specific expression patterns in Anopheles gambiae, with notable enrichment in salivary glands and chemosensory organs.
  • Expression is 10-45 fold higher in salivary glands compared to midgut tissues, suggesting roles in blood-feeding or pathogen transmission
  • Strong upregulation occurs in maxillary palps and antennae, indicating involvement in chemosensory functions
  • Blood-feeding triggers substantial downregulation in females (approximately 1.5-2 fold decrease during digestion and egg development)
  • Expression increases dramatically during late embryonic development and shows age-dependent decline in adults
  • Mating induces strong upregulation in the spermatheca but downregulation in reproductive tract tissues
The expression profile suggests this gene may function in host-seeking behavior and post-mating physiology.
