Summary

This gene exhibits dynamic expression patterns tied to reproductive biology and development in Anopheles gambiae.
  • Expression peaks dramatically in early embryos (0-4 hours post-fertilization, >93rd percentile) then declines, suggesting maternal transcript contribution or early zygotic roles
  • Strong upregulation occurs following blood meals, with sustained elevation through oogenesis and egg-laying (2.7-fold increase at 48h), and highest expression in gravid females compared to virgins or larvae
  • Tissue-specific enrichment is evident in ovaries, fat body, and salivary glands (40-fold higher than midgut), with moderate chemosensory organ expression favoring maxillary palps
  • Remarkably, the gene is completely silenced in cells infected with Wolbachia wAlB but maintained with wMel infection
The expression profile suggests potential roles in reproductive processes, embryonic development, and salivary gland function, possibly related to blood feeding physiology.
