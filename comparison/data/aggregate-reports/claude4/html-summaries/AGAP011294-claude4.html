Summary

This gene exhibits remarkable tissue-specific and temporal expression patterns across Anopheles gambiae biology. Most notably, it shows dramatic circadian oscillation in heads with 8-fold amplitude differences, while remaining constitutively high in body tissues. The gene demonstrates pronounced tissue specificity, with
  • highest expression in digestive tissues (proventriculus, anterior midgut)
  • strong upregulation in salivary glands versus midgut
  • elevated expression in male reproductive tissues
  • enhanced expression in maxillary palps versus antennae
. Additionally, the gene responds to physiological states including mating, blood feeding, and heat stress. The tissue-specific patterns and circadian regulation suggest potential roles in digestion, chemosensation, reproduction, and behavioral timing mechanisms.
