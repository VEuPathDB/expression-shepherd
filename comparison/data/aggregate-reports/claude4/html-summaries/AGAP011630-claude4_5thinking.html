Summary

This gene exhibits exceptional tissue-specific expression in Anopheles gambiae digestive and excretory organs.
  • Highest expression occurs in proventriculus (99.99th percentile), midgut (99.85th percentile in males), and Malpighian tubules (>99th percentile)
  • Also dramatically upregulated in testes (99.75th percentile) and salivary glands (99.88-99.94th percentile)
  • Expression is essentially absent in early embryos but dramatically increases during adult development, suggesting adult-specific metabolic or physiological functions
  • Shows modest responses to environmental stressors including heat shock, blood feeding, and Plasmodium infection
The extreme enrichment in digestive, excretory, and male reproductive tissues suggests potential roles in nutrient metabolism, waste processing, or ion transport.
