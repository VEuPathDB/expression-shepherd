Summary

This gene exhibits highly dynamic, context-dependent expression in Anopheles gambiae.
  • Development: Dramatic 65-fold upregulation at 48 hours post-egg laying, suggesting critical roles in late embryogenesis.
  • Stress response: Strong heat shock induction (up to 10.4 log2) in larvae with the 2La-standard chromosomal arrangement, but minimal response in 2La-inverted genotypes, indicating genotype-environment interactions.
  • Symbiont interactions: Strain-specific modulation by Wolbachia (2.2-fold increase with wMel, complete silencing with wAlB).
  • Reproductive biology: Progressive mating-induced upregulation in female reproductive tracts and enrichment in male testes.
  • Tissue distribution: Preferential expression in Malpighian tubules, midgut, and chemosensory organs, with age-dependent increases and circadian oscillation in body tissues.
The diverse expression patterns suggest roles in stress adaptation, development, and physiological homeostasis.
