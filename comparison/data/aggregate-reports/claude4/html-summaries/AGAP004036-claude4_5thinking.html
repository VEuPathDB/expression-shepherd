Summary

This gene exhibits a classic maternal effect expression pattern in Anopheles gambiae, with highly specific upregulation in female reproductive tissues. Key features include:
  • Over 100-fold enrichment in ovaries compared to male tissues and other female organs
  • High maternal deposition in freshly laid eggs (40-50 TPM at 0-2 hours) followed by rapid degradation during the first 8 hours of embryogenesis
  • Strong upregulation following blood meals, peaking at 48-72 hours post-feeding, coinciding with vitellogenesis
  • Dramatic translational activation during Plasmodium falciparum infection, shifting from non-polysomal to polysomal fractions
  • Virtual absence in males, larvae, and non-reproductive tissues
The expression profile strongly suggests a critical role in oogenesis, egg provisioning, and early embryonic development.
