Summary

Expression summary
  • In adult Anopheles gambiae, expression is strongly midgut‑biased, peaking in the posterior midgut and nearly absent from salivary glands and other tissues, with negligible antisense signal.
  • Midgut regulation is context‑dependent: P_* conditions robustly upregulate the transcript, and Plasmodium falciparum infection reduces total/non‑polysomal RNA while increasing polysomal association, suggesting enhanced translation.
  • Developmentally, a dramatic, transient burst occurs ~4–5 h after egg laying/early embryogenesis.
  • Within male reproductive organs, expression is testis‑enriched relative to accessory glands.
  • Whole‑body data indicate lower levels after blood feeding, consistent with broad physiological regulation.
