Highly expressed gene shows female-biased, tissue-specific patterns with salivary gland enrichment and circadian regulation

Summary

This gene exhibits complex spatiotemporal expression patterns in Anopheles gambiae.
  • Expression is strongly upregulated in adult females versus larvae and shows robust circadian oscillations with ~24-hour periodicity
  • Tissue-specific enrichment occurs in salivary glands (4-8 fold over midgut), fat body, and dramatically in male maxillary palps (9-fold over female palps)
  • Expression peaks in very early embryos (2h post-fertilization) then rapidly declines
  • The gene responds to physiological stimuli including 20-hydroxyecdysone (2-fold upregulation in reproductive tissues), blood feeding (2-fold whole-body increase), and ivermectin exposure (age-dependent upregulation)
  • Expression is downregulated 25-fold by wAlB Wolbachia infection
These patterns suggest roles in adult female physiology, chemosensation, circadian processes, and metabolic regulation.
