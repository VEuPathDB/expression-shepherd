Summary

This gene shows strong reproductive and blood meal-responsive expression in Anopheles gambiae females.
  • Expression is dramatically upregulated in gravid females compared to virgin females and larvae across multiple molecular forms
  • Following blood feeding, expression increases substantially at 24-48 hours post-blood meal and remains elevated through 15 days
  • Tissue-specific analysis reveals highest expression in ovaries and fat body, with minimal midgut expression
  • Early embryonic development shows peak expression at 2-4 hours post-fertilization, followed by decline
  • The gene demonstrates salivary gland enrichment (~46-fold higher than midgut) and maxillary palp enrichment over antennae
The expression pattern strongly suggests involvement in vitellogenesis, egg development, and female reproductive physiology following blood meal acquisition.
