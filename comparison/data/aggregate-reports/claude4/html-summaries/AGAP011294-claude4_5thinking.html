Summary

This gene exhibits remarkable tissue-specific expression in Anopheles gambiae digestive and secretory organs. Expression is exceptionally high in salivary glands (99.9th percentile), proventriculus (99.98th percentile), and midgut, while nearly absent in posterior midgut and ovaries. Developmentally, the gene shows >1000-fold upregulation from embryos to adults, with progressive increases through larval stages. Expression is also enriched in male testes and exhibits circadian oscillation in heads but constitutive high expression in bodies. The gene responds dynamically to blood feeding with transient downregulation followed by peak expression at 36 hours, and is progressively upregulated following mating in both male and female reproductive tissues. This expression pattern suggests potential roles in digestive physiology, salivary secretions, and reproductive processes.
