Summary

Female-biased, salivary gland–enriched transcript with light–dark and blood-feeding responses in Anopheles gambiae
  • Very high in adult females (whole body, carcass) and minimal in males and larvae; adult females (virgin > gravid) exceed larvae.
  • Salivary gland–enriched: hundreds to ~900 TPM in female salivary glands, midgut near baseline; tissue atlas confirms.
  • Rhythmic in bodies under light–dark and lower/flat in constant darkness; heads largely low.
  • Rapidly downregulated 3 h after a blood meal (partial rebound) and strongly induced by 20E in the atrium.
  • Pattern suggests a female salivary secretion involved in feeding/host interaction with anticipatory circadian control.
