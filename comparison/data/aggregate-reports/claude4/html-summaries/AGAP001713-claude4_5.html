AGAP001713 (stearoyl CoA desaturase)

Highly expressed in salivary glands and maxillary palps with strong male-biased and tissue-specific expression patterns

Summary

This gene exhibits exceptional tissue-specific and sex-biased expression in Anopheles gambiae.
  • Highest expression occurs in male maxillary palps (2118 TPM, 99.47th percentile), 3-fold higher than male whole body and 9-fold higher than antennae, indicating critical chemosensory function.
  • Salivary glands show 7-17-fold enrichment over midguts (400-951 TPM), particularly at timepoints coinciding with Plasmodium sporozoite invasion.
  • Female-biased expression in adults contrasts with male-biased larval expression.
  • Strong circadian rhythmicity with 4-hour periodicity suggests involvement in time-dependent behaviors.
  • Expression peaks in early embryogenesis (2h post-fertilization) then stabilizes.
The convergence of salivary gland enrichment, male maxillary palp specialization, and circadian regulation suggests roles in host-seeking behavior and pathogen transmission.
