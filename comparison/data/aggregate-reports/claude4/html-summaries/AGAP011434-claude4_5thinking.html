Summary

This gene exhibits highly stage-specific expression in Anopheles gambiae, with the most striking pattern being larval enrichment.
  • Expression in 4th instar larvae is 32-64 fold higher than in adults, with consistent upregulation across all larval instars (L1-L4) reaching 37-92 TPM while adult tissues show minimal expression (<1 TPM)
  • Within larvae, the gene shows posterior gut enrichment, with ~100-fold higher expression in posterior midgut and hindgut compared to anterior gut regions
  • In adult females, the gene displays a dramatic but transient 32-fold upregulation at 3 hours post-blood meal, returning to baseline by 24 hours
  • Additional transient expression occurs during late embryogenesis (43-46h post-fertilization), just before hatching
The larval-specific expression pattern and gut enrichment suggest this gene plays an essential role in larval digestive physiology or development, with a secondary function in adult blood meal processing.
