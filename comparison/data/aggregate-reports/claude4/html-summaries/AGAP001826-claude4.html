Summary

This gene demonstrates remarkably tissue-specific expression patterns with highest levels in salivary glands (>270 TPM) compared to other tissues like midguts (<5 TPM).
  • Shows dramatic developmental upregulation during Anopheles gambiae embryogenesis, increasing over 2.5-fold from early to mid-late stages
  • Exhibits strong feeding-responsive behavior with sustained upregulation following blood meals across multiple timepoints
  • Displays clear circadian rhythmicity and age-related expression changes in adults
  • Maintains consistently high expression in carcass tissues and chemosensory organs like maxillary palps
The extreme salivary gland enrichment combined with blood-feeding responsiveness suggests potential roles in vector-host interactions, possibly involving salivary protein secretion or pathogen transmission mechanisms.
