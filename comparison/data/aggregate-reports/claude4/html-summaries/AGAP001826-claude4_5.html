Highly Expressed in Somatic Tissues with Developmental Upregulation During Embryogenesis and Adult Aging

Summary

This gene exhibits dramatic developmental regulation and tissue-specific expression patterns in Anopheles gambiae.
  • During embryogenesis, expression increases sharply at 7-8 hours post-fertilization and remains elevated through late development, suggesting critical roles in post-blastoderm morphogenesis.
  • The gene shows strong age-dependent upregulation in adults, increasing 3-fold from emergence to day 10 and remaining high through day 30.
  • Expression is markedly enriched in somatic carcass tissues and salivary glands (>100-fold higher than midgut or reproductive organs), with particularly high levels in female salivary glands.
  • Blood feeding induces rapid upregulation, with peak expression at 3-6 hours post-feeding, especially in fat body tissue.
  • Expression is dramatically suppressed during pupal metamorphosis but recovers in adults.
The consistent high expression in metabolic and structural tissues suggests potential roles in energy metabolism, structural maintenance, or secretory functions critical for adult mosquito physiology.
