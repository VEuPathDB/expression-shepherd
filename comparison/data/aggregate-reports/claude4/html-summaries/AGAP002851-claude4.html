Summary

This gene exhibits highly tissue-specific expression in the Anopheles gambiae midgut, where it shows dramatic upregulation following blood meals but is severely suppressed during Plasmodium infection. Expression patterns include:
  • 13-fold increase at 24 hours post-blood meal with peak midgut expression
  • 100-fold reduction in parasite-infected samples compared to uninfected controls
  • Strong male bias in non-midgut tissues and age-related upregulation in males
  • Circadian regulation with tissue-specific oscillations
The gene demonstrates robust expression in multiple insecticide resistance studies and responds to various physiological stresses. Based on its midgut-specific expression pattern and blood meal responsiveness, this gene likely plays a critical role in blood digestion processes that are disrupted during malaria parasite development.
