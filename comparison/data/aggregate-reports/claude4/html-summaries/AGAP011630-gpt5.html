Salivary/gut-enriched, somatic-biased transcript responsive to feeding, hormones, and parasites in Anopheles gambiae

Summary

Summary
  • In Anopheles gambiae, expression is highest in digestive and secretory epithelia: salivary glands > midgut, with strong enrichment in the proventriculus and high levels in Malpighian tubules; heads and ovaries are low.
  • Adults show somatic bias (carcass >> reproductive tissues), adults > larvae, and embryos are induced late (~37–46 h). Testes are very high, while male accessory glands are low.
  • Regulation: blood feeding causes an early drop that later recovers; ivermectin counteracts the post-blood-meal decrease; mating downregulates the female lower reproductive tract, whereas 20E induces it; heads show a ZT12 spike.
  • Parasite exposure strongly upregulates transcripts in midgut (especially post-mating) and hemocytes.
  • Together, the pattern suggests an epithelial/secreted factor supporting digestive or barrier functions under hormonal and infection control.
