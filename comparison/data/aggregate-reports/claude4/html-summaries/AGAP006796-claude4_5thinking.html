Summary

This gene exhibits exceptional tissue specificity with predominant expression in the midgut and digestive tract of adult Anopheles gambiae mosquitoes, ranking in the 99th+ percentile across multiple tissue surveys and showing 100-fold higher expression compared to head tissues.
  • Expression is dramatically downregulated within 3 hours of blood feeding but recovers by 24 hours
  • The gene is adult-specific, being virtually absent in embryos and larvae, with age-dependent upregulation peaking at 20-30 days post-eclosion
  • Strong enrichment occurs in somatic body tissues and testes (98.64th percentile), with minimal expression in chemosensory organs, hemolymph, and ovaries
The transient blood meal suppression combined with constitutively high midgut expression suggests a role in digestive homeostasis or metabolic regulation that must be temporarily modulated during initial blood digestion.
