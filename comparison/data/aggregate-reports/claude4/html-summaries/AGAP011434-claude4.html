Summary

This gene exhibits dramatic developmental stage-specific expression in Anopheles gambiae, being highly expressed in larval stages (particularly L3-4 instars with 60-90+ TPM) but substantially downregulated in adults (<2 TPM). The gene shows rapid upregulation within 3 hours of blood feeding, reaching the 94th percentile before returning to baseline, suggesting involvement in early blood digestion or reproductive physiology.
  • Strong tissue-specific expression favoring posterior gut regions and midgut over anterior digestive tissues
  • Minimal expression differences between molecular forms or sexes within developmental stages
  • Consistent downregulation following mating in females
The expression patterns suggest this gene likely functions in larval development and metabolic processes associated with blood meal processing.
