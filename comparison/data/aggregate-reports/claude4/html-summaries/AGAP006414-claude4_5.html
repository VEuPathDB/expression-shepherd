Summary

This gene demonstrates remarkable tissue-specific expression in the midgut and female-biased expression patterns across multiple experiments.
  • Expression is exceptionally high in the midgut (>98th percentile), particularly in the posterior midgut (2281 TPM), with 5-fold higher levels than salivary glands
  • Shows strong female bias with approximately 2.6-fold higher expression in females versus males
  • Exhibits dynamic blood meal response with dramatic downregulation at 3 hours post-feeding followed by recovery
  • Upregulated in insecticide-resistant populations and mated females' spermathecae
  • Highly enriched in testes (96th percentile, 40-fold higher than accessory glands)
The consistent high expression in digestive tissues combined with blood meal responsiveness suggests potential roles in blood digestion, gut physiology, or nutrient metabolism.
