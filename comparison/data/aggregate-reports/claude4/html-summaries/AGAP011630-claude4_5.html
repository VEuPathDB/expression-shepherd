Summary

This gene exhibits extreme tissue-specific expression patterns in Anopheles gambiae, with particularly dramatic enrichment in the digestive tract and salivary glands.
  • Expression is extraordinarily high in the proventriculus (>100,000 TPM, 99.99th percentile) and anterior midgut regions, suggesting specialized roles in anterior digestive function
  • Salivary glands show 13-fold higher expression than midgut tissue, indicating potential involvement in blood-feeding or pathogen transmission biology
  • The gene displays robust developmental regulation, being virtually absent in early embryos but dramatically upregulated in adults (>1000 TPM)
  • Male testes show testis-enriched expression (~137-fold higher than accessory glands), suggesting additional roles in spermatogenesis
The consistently high percentile rankings (>95th percentile) across diverse adult tissues indicate this gene encodes an abundant protein critical for mosquito physiology, likely involved in digestive secretion, immune defense, or tissue-specific metabolic processes.
