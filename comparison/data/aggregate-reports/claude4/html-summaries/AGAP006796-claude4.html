Summary

This gene exhibits highly tissue-specific expression patterns with strongest activity in digestive tissues, particularly the midgut where expression exceeds the 99th percentile. Expression shows dramatic developmental regulation, being substantially higher in adults compared to larvae (~2.5-fold difference). The gene demonstrates consistent sex-independent expression within tissues and responds to physiological states including blood feeding with transient downregulation at 3 hours post-feeding. Given its extremely high expression in gut tissues, moderate expression in salivary glands, and developmental upregulation in adults, this gene likely functions in digestive processes or nutrient metabolism critical for adult Anopheles gambiae physiology.
