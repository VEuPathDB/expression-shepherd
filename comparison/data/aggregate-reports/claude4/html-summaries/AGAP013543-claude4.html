Summary

This gene exhibits extremely tissue-specific expression patterns in Anopheles gambiae, with particularly high expression in
  • adult somatic tissues (carcass, salivary glands, testes) showing >1000-fold higher expression than reproductive tissues or larvae
  • specialized digestive structures like the proventriculus and anterior midgut
  • pupal stages compared to adults
. The gene demonstrates dramatic developmental regulation, transitioning from high pupal expression to adult-specific patterns, and shows clear sexual dimorphism in several tissues including salivary glands and reproductive organs. The consistent tissue-specific patterns across multiple experiments suggest this gene likely encodes a protein with specialized functions in adult mosquito physiology, particularly in digestive processes and somatic tissue maintenance, rather than reproductive or neurological functions.
