Summary

This gene exhibits highly female-specific expression with dramatic upregulation in reproductive tissues, particularly ovaries where expression reaches 30-fold higher levels than in female carcass.
  • Shows peak expression during early embryogenesis (0-4 hours post-egg laying) followed by rapid decline
  • Responds strongly to blood feeding with sustained upregulation from 24-72 hours post-meal
  • Exhibits tissue-specific expression in fat body and enhanced translational activity during Plasmodium falciparum infection
  • Demonstrates strain-specific responses to Wolbachia infection in cell lines
The expression pattern strongly suggests this gene plays a critical role in female reproductive biology, potentially involved in oogenesis, early embryonic development, and the physiological response to blood feeding required for egg maturation.
