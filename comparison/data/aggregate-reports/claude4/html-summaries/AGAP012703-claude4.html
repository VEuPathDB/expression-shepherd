Summary

This gene exhibits consistently high expression levels (typically >95th percentile) across multiple Anopheles gambiae tissues and developmental stages, with several distinctive expression patterns.
  • Shows strong male-biased expression in somatic tissues with 2-4 fold differences
  • Displays robust circadian oscillation peaking during dark phases
  • Demonstrates clear blood meal responsiveness with downregulation following feeding
  • Exhibits tissue-specific expression being highly expressed in chemosensory organs, heads, and fat body but reduced in reproductive tissues
The gene's high expression in chemosensory organs, circadian regulation, and blood meal responsiveness suggest potential roles in olfactory processing, metabolic regulation, or feeding behavior, particularly given its male-biased expression pattern which may relate to host-seeking differences between sexes.
