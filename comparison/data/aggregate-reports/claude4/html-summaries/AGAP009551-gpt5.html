Midgut and testes enrichment with circadian and desiccation responses in Anopheles gambiae

Summary

Expression overview in Anopheles gambiae:
  • Marked tissue bias: high in midgut (posterior midgut enriched), Malpighian tubules (male > female), and testes (>> accessory glands); low in heads, antennae, and salivary glands.
  • Strong temporal control: large-amplitude circadian/diel oscillations in bodies; sharp developmental peaks around 6–10 h of embryogenesis and 7–8 h post-oviposition.
  • Condition responses: desiccation induces 3–5×; during Plasmodium infection, total midgut mRNA falls while polysome association rises (indicative of translational upregulation).
  • Population variation: consistently higher in S-form than M-form, strongest in virgin females.
  • Together, these patterns suggest roles in gut/renal physiology and male reproduction under circadian and stress regulation.
