Summary

Expression overview in Anopheles gambiae
  • Very high, midgut-enriched expression across adult tissues (≈98–99th percentile), with lower levels in salivary glands and many other tissues; small RNA reads are also midgut-biased.
  • Adult-biased overall: adults (especially female carcass) ≫ larvae; testes are strongly expressed relative to male accessory glands.
  • Feeding dynamics: sharp downregulation within hours of a normal blood meal, rebounding by ~24 h; ivermectin-treated meals restore/elevate expression.
  • Post-mating regulation: spermatheca up after mating and 20E; lower reproductive tract shows a transient 3–24 h switch.
  • Circadian control: ~4-fold rhythmicity in bodies with weaker head oscillation.
  • Infection context: in midgut, Plasmodium falciparum reduces total RNA but increases polysome association; hemocytes and invasion stages show higher levels.
