Gut- and salivary-enriched, circadian and feeding-responsive expression in Anopheles gambiae

Model: GPT-5
  • Public gene page (expression graphs table for manual perusal)
  • Internal gene page (interactive version of the AI summary below, including co-visualisation of raw data to aid validation)

Summary

Expression profile in Anopheles gambiae:
  • Very high in adult midgut and salivary glands; enriched in anterior midgut; strongly elevated in larval gastric caeca.
  • High in hemolymph, carcass and head/thorax; weak in ovaries and male accessory glands; testes show clear enrichment.
  • Displays robust circadian cycling with higher night-time expression in heads and bodies.
  • Feeding-modulated: early post-bloodmeal suppression with rebound (and antennal induction at 36–48 h); ivermectin increases expression versus blood-fed controls.
  • Elevated with infectious Plasmodium in hemocytes; midguts show a shift toward polysome association during infection.
  • Strongly downregulated by Wolbachia in cultured cells; increases with adult age; modest responses to heat shock and severe desiccation.
