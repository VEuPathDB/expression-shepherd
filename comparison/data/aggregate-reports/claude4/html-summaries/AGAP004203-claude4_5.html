AGAP004203 (vitellogenin)

Dramatic Female-Biased and Blood Meal-Induced Expression in Anopheles gambiae

Model: Claude Sonnet 4.5

Summary

This gene exhibits striking female-specific expression across nearly all tissues, with 8-10 log2 fold differences between sexes, and undergoes massive (>1000-fold) upregulation following blood feeding.
  • In non-blood-fed mosquitoes, expression is consistently high in female tissues (96-99th percentiles) but minimal in males (15-59th percentiles), suggesting a fundamental role in female physiology
  • Blood meal ingestion triggers exceptional induction peaking at 24 hours post-feeding (99th percentile) in whole body, fat body, midgut, and even antennae
  • Expression patterns indicate involvement in blood digestion, nutrient metabolism, or vitellogenesis processes
  • The gene also responds to mating with a transient peak at 6 hours post-mating and is downregulated by 20-hydroxyecdysone
The combined sex-biased and blood meal-responsive expression strongly suggests this gene encodes a female-specific factor critical for blood meal processing and reproduction, possibly a vitellogenin or related yolk protein.
