Summary

Summary
  • In adult Anopheles gambiae, expression is extremely enriched in salivary glands and the anterior gut (proventriculus ≫ anterior midgut ≫ posterior midgut), and broadly high in somatic tissues.
  • There is marked male and maxillary palp bias; head is lower overall but displays strong circadian oscillation (peaks CT12–16).
  • Reproduction‑linked regulation: strongly testis‑biased and induced post‑mating in testes and in the female lower reproductive tract.
  • Responsive to infection and hormones: sharply upregulated with Plasmodium exposure and ecdysteroid; hemocytes are higher with infectious parasites.
  • Dynamic after blood meals/HMBPP and with age and stress.
  • These patterns suggest a highly abundant secreted salivary/gut factor involved in digestion or barrier immunity, with additional roles in reproductive physiology.
