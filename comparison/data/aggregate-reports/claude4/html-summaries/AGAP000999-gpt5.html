Summary

Expression overview in Anopheles gambiae
  • Enriched in female reproductive tissues, especially ovaries; rises at/just after oviposition, and early embryos peak at 2–4 h then decline.
  • Adults (gravid > virgin) exceed larvae; after a blood meal there is a 3 h dip followed by a sustained 24–96 h increase (peak ~48 h).
  • Tissue bias: high in salivary glands and maxillary palps (male-biased among palps), low in midgut and testes.
  • Pathogen/endo-symbiont effects: in infected midguts, polysome association increases while total mRNA falls; Wolbachia wAlb eliminates expression in Sua5B cells.
  • Together, patterns suggest functions in female reproduction and salivary/chemosensory biology, responsive to feeding and infection.
