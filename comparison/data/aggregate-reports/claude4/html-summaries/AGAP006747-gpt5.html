Summary

Expression overview
  • Moderate–high expression across Anopheles gambiae tissues; testis-biased versus male accessory gland and broad baseline in adults and larvae.
  • Reproductive cues: mating elevates female midgut transcripts while 20-hydroxyecdysone suppresses; a marked surge occurs at 48 h post‑oviposition.
  • Infection: in midgut, Plasmodium falciparum reduces total/non‑polysomal RNA but increases polysome association, suggesting enhanced translation despite lower abundance.
  • Stress/age: heat shock responses diverge by 2La arrangement (up in standard, down in inverted); expression rises ~2–3× with adult age.
  • Ecology: Wolbachia effects are strain‑specific in Sua5B cells (wMel up, wAlB absent). Tissue trends include higher salivary‑gland than midgut levels and male‑biased enrichment in olfactory appendages.
