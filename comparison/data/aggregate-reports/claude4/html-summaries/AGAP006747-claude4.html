Summary

This gene exhibits highly tissue-specific expression patterns across Anopheles gambiae, with particularly strong enrichment in
  • chemosensory organs (maxillary palps and antennae)
  • reproductive tissues of both sexes
  • salivary glands compared to midgut
. Expression shows clear sexual dimorphism in chemosensory appendages, favoring males, and displays developmental regulation with peak expression at 48 hours post-egg laying. Notably, the gene is substantially downregulated in deltamethrin-resistant mosquitoes and shows strain-specific responses to Wolbachia infection. The consistent high expression in chemosensory and reproductive tissues, combined with post-mating upregulation, suggests potential roles in olfactory processing and reproductive physiology.
