Highly expressed, male-biased chemosensory gene with circadian regulation and tissue-specific enrichment in antennae and maxillary palps

Model: Claude Sonnet 4.5

Summary

This gene exhibits pronounced tissue-specific and sex-biased expression patterns in Anopheles gambiae.
  • Highest expression occurs in chemosensory appendages (antennae: 1012-2308 TPM; maxillary palps: 2138-2308 TPM) compared to whole body tissues (43-354 TPM)
  • Strong male-biased expression across nearly all tissues, with 2-fold higher levels in male heads, carcass, and midgut
  • Displays robust circadian oscillation peaking at subjective midday in heads
  • Downregulated following blood feeding, particularly at 3-72 hours post-meal
  • Highest expression in fat body tissue and consistently elevated across adult life stages
The chemosensory enrichment, male-biased expression, and circadian rhythmicity suggest involvement in olfactory-mediated host-seeking behavior and sensory perception critical for mosquito ecology.
