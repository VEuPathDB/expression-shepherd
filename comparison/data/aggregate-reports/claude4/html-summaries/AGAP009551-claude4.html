Summary

This gene exhibits pronounced tissue-specific expression patterns across multiple Anopheles gambiae experiments.
  • Highest expression consistently occurs in digestive tissues, particularly Malpighian tubules and midgut regions
  • Shows dramatic sexual dimorphism with female-biased expression in whole body samples
  • Displays robust circadian oscillations in body tissues while remaining low in heads
  • Responds dynamically to physiological states including desiccation stress, blood feeding, and mating
The gene's strong enrichment in excretory (Malpighian tubules) and digestive (midgut) tissues, combined with upregulation during desiccation stress and downregulation following blood feeding, suggests potential involvement in osmoregulation and metabolic homeostasis.
