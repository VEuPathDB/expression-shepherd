Summary

Expression overview in Anopheles gambiae:
  • Extremely high in adult somatic tissues (carcass/whole body) with strong upregulation relative to larvae and heads, and little circadian cycling.
  • Pronounced localization to the digestive system: massive levels in proventriculus/anterior midgut and strong enrichment in salivary glands (3–12× over midgut); midgut ranks among the highest tissues.
  • Dynamic regulation: transient suppression ~3 h after a blood meal; modest increases with ivermectin; robust induction by 20‑hydroxyecdysone in the spermatheca and atrium; increased under Plasmodium challenge and in infected hemocytes.
  • One dataset shows extreme testis enrichment, while another reports depletion in male reproductive tissues, indicating study‑specific differences.
  • Given foregut/salivary dominance and feeding/parasite responsiveness, the transcript likely encodes a highly expressed secreted or barrier protein involved in digestion or host–parasite interface.
