Highly expressed midgut-specific gene with strong adult and female enrichment in Anopheles gambiae

Summary

This gene exhibits extremely high, tissue-specific expression in the midgut of Anopheles gambiae, consistently ranking above the 99th percentile across multiple studies.
  • Expression is dramatically enriched in adult stages compared to embryonic and larval stages, with particularly strong female bias
  • Within adults, midgut expression is 2-3 fold higher than salivary glands and substantially elevated compared to reproductive tissues and heads
  • The gene shows transient downregulation at 3 hours post-blood meal, followed by rapid recovery, suggesting involvement in blood meal processing
  • Expression increases with mosquito age (20-30 days post-eclosion) and is enriched in body/carcass tissues rather than chemosensory appendages
The midgut-specific, adult-enriched expression pattern suggests this gene encodes a digestive enzyme or metabolic protein critical for nutrient processing in female mosquitoes.
