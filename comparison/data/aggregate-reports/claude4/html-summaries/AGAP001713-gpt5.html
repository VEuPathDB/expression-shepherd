AGAP001713 (stearoyl CoA desaturase)

Broad, high-level expression with tissue enrichments, circadian cycling, and feeding/infection responsiveness in Anopheles gambiae

Summary

Overview: This transcript is abundantly and broadly expressed in Anopheles gambiae, with pronounced temporal, tissue, and environmental modulation.
  • Development: Very high at 2 h embryos then rapidly decays; adults exceed larvae, and oviposition shows an early peak followed by rebound.
  • Tissues: Enriched in carcass and salivary glands; high in testes (vs accessory glands) and strongly male‑palp biased; lower in some reproductive tissues; larval anterior midgut is highest.
  • Physiology: Robust circadian cycling (early‑night peaks); generally induced after blood feeding, modestly suppressed by ecdysone, and elevated by ivermectin at 6 days.
  • Infection/translation: Parasite infection shifts RNA toward polysomes and expression declines after invasion.
  • Cell lines: Strong downregulation with Wolbachia wAlB and densovirus.
These patterns suggest a core physiological role modulated by feeding status and the circadian clock.
