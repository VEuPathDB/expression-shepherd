Summary

This gene exhibits pronounced tissue-specific expression in Anopheles gambiae, with highest levels in midgut (99th percentile) and salivary glands (99th percentile), suggesting roles in digestion or secretion.
  • Expression shows robust circadian oscillations and is strongly upregulated in adults compared to immature stages, with female-biased expression
  • The gene displays dramatic 50-fold downregulation upon Wolbachia wMel infection and dynamic temporal responses to blood feeding, including late-phase upregulation at 36-48 hours post-feeding
  • Expression is associated with insecticide resistance phenotypes and responds to environmental stressors including heat shock and desiccation
  • High expression in hemocytes and modulation during Plasmodium infection suggest potential immune or metabolic functions
The consistent enrichment in digestive tissues, blood feeding responsiveness, and pathogen-related modulation suggest this gene may participate in metabolic or digestive processes critical for mosquito physiology and vector competence.
