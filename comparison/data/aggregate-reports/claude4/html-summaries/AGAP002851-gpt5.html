AGAP002851 (Niemann-Pick C2 protein)

Midgut‑enriched, blood meal–responsive transcript with circadian and hormonal control in Anopheles gambiae

Summary

Summary.
  • Marked enrichment in the adult midgut of Anopheles gambiae, especially posterior midgut; salivary glands, heads, and chemosensory appendages are near background; transcription is sense-specific.
  • Strong induction after blood feeding (early rise by 3 h; sharp peak near 24 h), replicated across array and RNA‑Seq data.
  • Reproductive regulation: transient upregulation 2–6 h post‑mating and clear elevation with ecdysone; virgin females exceed gravid females.
  • Robust circadian cycling in bodies under LD and DD, with low, stable head expression.
  • Sex/age effects: male‑biased somatic expression with age‑related increase; testes > accessory glands in one dataset.
  • Desiccation stress suppresses expression.
  • Together, the pattern suggests a midgut epithelial factor involved in post‑blood‑meal digestion or epithelial remodeling under steroid control.
