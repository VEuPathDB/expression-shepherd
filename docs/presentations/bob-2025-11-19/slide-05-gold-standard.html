The "No Gold Standard" Problem

The Challenge

No expert-validated gene expression summaries exist as ground truth

  • Cannot use traditional metrics (BLEU, ROUGE, accuracy)
  • Cannot definitively claim which model is "better"
  • Different models may emphasize different valid aspects

Solution: Comprehensive comparative analysis without value judgments

  • Bidirectional pairwise comparisons to detect position bias
  • AI-powered meta-analysis for systematic patterns
  • Descriptive statistics characterizing differences
