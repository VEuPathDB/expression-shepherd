Gene Selection: 20 "Favourite Genes"

Source: George Christophides mosquito immunity lab, Imperial College

Gene Categories

Immunity & Defense:

  • PGRP-LB (immune recognition)
  • Cecropin (antimicrobial)
  • Defensin (antimicrobial)
  • TOLL5A, REL2 (innate immunity)

Physiology:

  • Vitellogenin, Lipophorin
  • Saglin (salivary protein)
  • Chitinase, Peritrophin

Why These Genes?

  • Strong focus on mosquito immunity and vector biology
  • Diverse expression patterns across tissues and conditions
  • Well-studied genes with complex profiles
  • Good test cases for robust comparison

Dataset

20 VectorBase genes (Anopheles gambiae) with full expression profiles across multiple experiments
